Executive Summary
Infrabyx proposes to deliver Client Name multi-tenant, cloud-hosted Loan Management System (LMS), Finbyx, covering the complete lending lifecycle, including borrower onboarding, origination and underwriting, servicing, collections and recovery, an embedded general ledger, and full RBI regulatory and Credit Information Company reporting which is accessible entirely through a browser, with a field application for on-ground and collections staff.
The platform ships pre-built with a catalogue of 33 loan products spanning secured, unsecured, trade finance, MSME/priority-sector, and emerging digital-lending categories, and is designed to be configuration-driven: new products, form fields, workflows, pricing and regulatory returns are set up as data, not code, so [Client Name]’s business teams can evolve the product suite without waiting on a development release cycle.
Compliance is treated as a first-class design requirement rather than an afterthought. The system is built to RBI's Scale-Based Regulation (SBR) reporting framework, CIC/bureau reporting standards, PMLA/AML obligations, the Digital Lending Directions (Key Fact Statement, APR disclosure, cooling-off), co-lending and securitization accounting, and data protection under the DPDP Act  giving [Client Name] a platform that is audit-ready from day one.
Understanding Client Name’s Requirements
Lending institutions in India today are expected to launch products faster than their legacy systems allow, while regulatory scrutiny  from RBI's SBR framework, Digital Lending Directions, and PMLA obligations, to CIC reporting and the DPDP Act  continues to increase in both scope and granularity. Common challenges we hear from NBFCs and banks include:
	•	Product launches held back by hard-coded loan systems that require a development cycle for every new scheme, rate change, or eligibility rule.
	•	Fragmented systems for origination, servicing, collections, and accounting that create reconciliation gaps and slow month-end close.
	•	Manual or semi-automated regulatory returns (RBI, CIC, FIU-IND) that are error-prone and consume disproportionate compliance-team effort.
	•	Difficulty producing the Key Fact Statement and APR disclosures the Digital Lending Directions require, particularly for LSP/fintech-sourced loans.
	•	Limited visibility into portfolio risk, delinquency, and collection efficiency across products and branches in real time.
FINBYX is architected specifically to close these gaps for an NBFC or bank of [Client Name] scale.
Proposed Solution: Platform Overview
Deployment Model
The LMS is delivered as a multi-tenant, cloud-hosted SaaS application. A single codebase and infrastructure stack serves [Client Name]if required in future, additional group entities or branches through logically isolated tenant partitions. Access is entirely through a web browser (desktop and responsive mobile), with no client-side installation required. A companion Progressive Web App (PWA) view is available for field staff such as collections agents, gold appraisers, and valuers who need offline-capable data capture.
Layered, Configuration-Driven Architecture
The platform is organised into five logical layers, each communicating with the one below only through defined APIs, so any layer's underlying technology can be changed without disrupting the others. The stack is described at the module/API/data-model level rather than tied to a specific programming language, allowing your IT teams to select the technology (microservices or modular monolith, any modern RDBMS, any cloud provider) that best fits in-house skills and data-residency requirements.
Critically, new products, form fields, workflows, interest and fee rules, and regulatory returns are configured as data rather than hard-coded so the business can launch and adjust products without a software release.

Regulatory & Compliance Assurance
Compliance is engineered into the platform's architecture, not layered on afterward. Regulatory thresholds and formats reflect the extant RBI Master Directions and statutes, and should be re-validated by [Client Name]'s Compliance function before go-live.
Compliance Area
How the Platform Addresses It
RBI Scale-Based Regulation (SBR) Reporting
Configurable rules-and-templates engine (not hard-coded returns) so the applicable layer, thresholds, and CRILC/CIMS return formats can be updated as RBI revises the SBR framework.
CIC / Bureau Reporting
Monthly submission to CIBIL, Equifax, Experian, and CRIF High Mark in the Uniform Credit Reporting Format.
AML / CFT & PMLA
Dedicated Principal Officer role with an STR/CTR review queue and FIU-IND submission sign-off; alerts, decisions, and acknowledgements retained in an immutable case file for inspection.
Digital Lending Directions (KFS, APR, Cooling-Off)
Key Fact Statement and APR disclosures generated at loan agreement stage for every retail and MSME loan; LSP/fintech-sourced loans tracked to stay within RBI's permitted co-lending/outsourcing structure.
Co-Lending, Assignment & Securitization
Modelled as its own module with dedicated accounting treatment and RBI/CIC reporting, including NBS and board-disclosure line items.
Data Privacy (DPDP Act)
Data privacy controls mapped to the DPDP Act, alongside security and cyber-resilience controls aligned to the RBI IT Framework for NBFCs.
Provisioning & Prudential Ratios
Ind AS 109 Expected Credit Loss (ECL) and IRAC provisioning, with prudential ratios including CRAR and leverage available for regulatory and board reporting.
Access Control & Audit
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) at module, screen, and field level, layered with branch/region scoping, and a full audit trail.
